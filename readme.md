Furniture adds a boatload of new furniture items, some you've seen before in other furniture mods, and some you've never seen before. Polish, style, and dress your home the way *you* want.

<br>

Make your house a home. Leave your comfort zone.

<br>

Furniture is a mod that came along with Satisfyl's desire for intricate decor, something that does more than look pretty and it serves to liven up your home by adding a variety of functional or interesting decorative elements.

<br>

## All the Colors

<br>

- Stylish curtains available in all 16 colors  
- Clocks, mirrors, and furniture sets available in all vanilla woodtypes  
- Sofas, pouffes, and lamps available in all 16 colors  

<br>

Just a little to the left honey. No, my left, not your left!

<br>

## Modular Furniture

<br>

- Connect dressers of any woodtype to a desk of any woodtype to create your own personalized desk  
- Increase the length of your lamps from a tiny desk lamp to a tall standing lamp  
- Cover your windows with shutters of any length  

<br>

There's so much diversity, it's almost like we're at an IKEA!

<br>

## Other Decorative Elements

<br>

- Differently styled crafting stations that fit in just a little more compared to your original crafting table  
- Terrarium for some miniature greenery in your home  
- Displays to show off your most prized possessions  
- An aquarium capable of housing pufferfish, cod, or salmon  
- A ship in a bottle to make you feel like you're out at sea  
- A grammophone that can play your favorite music discs on repeat  
- A trashcan to dispose of any unwanted friends... I mean, items. Definitely items. Also remember that one man's trash is another man's treasure
